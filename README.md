## The entry

> Sorry, I'll miss the sync — I'm stuck in a meeting about cutting meetings, and it just got extended.

One line, 100 characters (gate: 1–200). `main.ts` (Node 22) writes it to
`/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable
is set), byte for byte, with no trailing newline.

## How it maps to the judge's brief (in weight order)

1. **On topic: it is an excuse for skipping a meeting** — addressed to the
   sync being missed, with a concrete reason, not a general joke about work.
2. **Surprising turn** — the reason is a meeting about cutting meetings:
   the cure for meetings is itself the thing keeping him from the meeting.
3. **Office relatability** — everyone has sat in a meeting about having
   fewer meetings, and "it just got extended" is the universal ending.
4. **Compact** — 100 of 200 characters; the whole joke in one breath.
5. **Fresh angle** — the excuse indicts the system, not the speaker: he is
   not lazy, he is trapped inside the company's own anti-meeting program.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
